PATEL PLY &
HARDWARE SANITARY

Founded nearly two decades ago in Amreli, Patel Ply & Hardware Sanitary was built on a singular conviction: that regional builders, architects, and discerning homeowners deserve direct access to the finest architectural materials without compromising on genuine standards.

What began as a dedicated timber depot has evolved into Saurashtra’s leading architectural materials showroom. Today, our Manekpara gallery houses over 4,000 carefully curated references—spanning IS:710 Marine-grade calibrated plywood, European acoustic mortise locks, solid brass architectural pull handles, monolithic stone sanitaryware, and precision-engineered CP water fixtures.

01

Certified Core Integrity

Every sheet of plywood is strictly IS:710 or IS:303 certified with zero-gap core calibration, vacuum-pressured preservative treatment, and boiling water proof phenolic bonds.

02

Micro-Mechanical Precision

We partner with established hardware masters to supply 3D-adjustable concealed hinges, magnetic acoustic latches, and solid brass forged handles tested for 200,000+ open-close cycles.

03

Tactile Water Architecture

Our bath fittings pair physical vapor deposition (PVD) finishes in brushed bronze and gunmetal with Swiss Neoperl aerators and ceramic disc cartridges for silent, splash-free flow.

04

Direct Factory Assurance

No intermediaries, no imitation batches. We provide genuine brand warranties, comprehensive technical specification sheets, and on-site material testing for architectural studios.
